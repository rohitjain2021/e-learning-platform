MAPLE GROVE TEACHER PATHWAY - MENTOR REVIEW AND ESCALATION
Document ID: DEMO-04
Version: 1.0
Classification: Fictional demonstration content; shared with all demo users.
This is fictional sample content, not a real organizational or licensure policy.

REVIEW OWNERSHIP
The assigned mentor reviews a learner's hours and competency evidence. Mentors
review only learners assigned to them. Learners cannot approve their own work.
The program administrator manages mentor assignments and unresolved issues.

REVIEW TARGET
The mentor aims to review submitted hours and evidence within two business days.
Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding the placement's holidays.
The learner can see a submission's status while waiting for review.

POSSIBLE OUTCOMES
Approved means the mentor has accepted an hours entry. Signed Off means the
mentor has accepted a competency. Changes Requested means the learner must fix
the submission and resubmit it. Rejected means the submitted activity is not
accepted. Pending means no review decision has been recorded.

CORRECTION PROCESS
For Changes Requested, the mentor explains what is missing. The learner corrects
the original submission and adds a response explaining the update. The mentor
reviews it again. Resubmission does not make an entry approved automatically.
Keep the original submission and review history available for audit.

WHEN TO ESCALATE
If an entry remains pending for more than three business days, the learner sends
the submission ID to the program administrator through the demo support form.
The administrator contacts the mentor or assigns a replacement reviewer within
one business day. Escalation does not automatically approve hours or competencies.

MENTOR ABSENCE
If the assigned mentor is unavailable, the administrator assigns an authorized
replacement. Learners should not change the reviewer themselves or submit the
same activity again to bypass the queue. Administrative reassignment should be
recorded with the reason and the identity of the person making the change.
