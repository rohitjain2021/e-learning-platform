MAPLE GROVE TEACHER PATHWAY - HOURS AND SUBMISSIONS
Document ID: DEMO-02
Version: 1.0
Classification: Fictional demonstration content; shared with all demo users.
This is fictional sample content, not a real organizational or licensure policy.

HOURS RULES
The fictional program requires 240 approved hours of supervised classroom
practice. Learners should plan for about 20 eligible hours each week over 12
weeks. Actual approved hours determine progress; the weekly plan is not approval.

WHAT COUNTS
Eligible activities include supporting a supervised lesson, observing classroom
instruction with a mentor, and helping with classroom assessment activities.
The placement must be active, and the mentor must be able to confirm the work.
Only time spent on eligible supervised activities may be submitted.

WHAT DOES NOT COUNT
Travel, lunch breaks, orientation, unsupervised preparation, and online coursework
do not count toward the classroom practice target. Coursework can support learning
but must not be added to classroom practice hours. Do not count the same time twice.

HOW TO SUBMIT
For each entry, record the activity date, duration, activity category, a short
description, and the assigned mentor. Submit weekly entries by Friday at 5 p.m.
in the placement's local time. Do not include student names, student records,
photographs of children, or other personal information in descriptions.

HOW HOURS BECOME APPROVED
A newly submitted entry has Pending status. The mentor aims to review it within
two business days. Only Approved entries count toward the practice target.
Changes Requested entries must be corrected and resubmitted; they do not count
until approved. Rejected entries also do not count.

CORRECTIONS AND DELAYS
Explain the change when correcting an entry. If a mistake is found after approval,
ask the administrator to reopen the entry; do not silently replace its history.
For pending entries older than three business days, follow the escalation process
in 04-mentor-review-and-escalation.txt.
