MAPLE GROVE TEACHER PATHWAY - PROGRAM OVERVIEW
Document ID: DEMO-01
Version: 1.0
Classification: Fictional demonstration content; shared with all demo users.
This is fictional sample content, not a real organizational or licensure policy.

PROGRAM FACTS
Organization: Maple Grove Learning Partnership, a fictional organization.
Program: Maple Grove Teacher Pathway.
Length: 12 weeks.
Practice target: 240 approved hours of supervised classroom practice.
Competency target: Four signed-off competencies.
These targets are invented for this demo. They are not teacher certification,
accreditation, or state licensing requirements.

PURPOSE
The program helps classroom support staff practice teaching skills while working
with a mentor. The assistant answers questions using the program documents.
It does not decide whether someone is qualified to teach.

GETTING STARTED
The program administrator invites the learner and assigns a mentor. The learner
accepts the invitation, checks the program assignment, and completes orientation.
The administrator then activates the placement. Classroom practice can be logged
only after placement activation. Orientation itself does not count as practice.

WHO DOES WHAT
Learners submit practice hours and evidence of their skills. Mentors review hours
and assess evidence for learners assigned to them. The program administrator
manages invitations, assignments, escalations, and completion checks.

COMPLETION
Finishing the 12-week period alone does not complete the program. The learner
needs 240 approved practice hours, all four competency sign-offs, and a final
administrator review. Pending hours and unreviewed evidence do not satisfy these
requirements. Completion of this fictional program is not a teaching license.
