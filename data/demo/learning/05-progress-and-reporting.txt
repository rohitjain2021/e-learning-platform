SNAPSHOT SCOPE
This is a fixed example snapshot at the end of program week 9. It does not update
automatically. An answer about this record must say it refers to the demo snapshot,
rather than implying it is a live report or a query of production learner data.

HOW TO INTERPRET THE SNAPSHOT
DEMO-001 has 60 approved hours remaining and has completed 75 percent of the
approved-hours target. The 20 pending hours do not count toward completion.
If all 20 pending hours are approved, the approved total becomes 200 and the
remaining requirement becomes 40. That is a hypothetical outcome, not an approval.
The learner has three of four competency sign-offs and is not yet complete.

PROGRAM REPORTING
A program progress report includes approved hours, pending hours, competency
sign-offs, outstanding reviews, and completion review status. Keep approved and
pending totals separate. For this demo, the administrator prepares the report
after the weekly review cycle. This document defines no funding eligibility rules.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE
The snapshot does not establish a teaching license, funding eligibility, payment
entitlement, or a guaranteed completion date. Questions about those topics need
additional authoritative documents or a responsible program administrator.
