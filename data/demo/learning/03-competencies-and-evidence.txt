MAPLE GROVE TEACHER PATHWAY - COMPETENCIES AND EVIDENCE

Classification: Fictional demonstration content; shared with all demo users.
This is fictional sample content, not a real organizational or licensure policy.

ASSESSMENT RULE
The fictional program has four competencies. Each requires evidence and an
explicit mentor sign-off. Approved practice hours do not automatically approve a
competency. A learner must meet both the hours target and competency requirements.

C1: LESSON PLANNING
The learner prepares a lesson with a learning objective, activities, materials,
and a way to check understanding. Evidence is one anonymized lesson plan and
mentor feedback. The mentor checks that the activities match the objective.

C2: CLASSROOM ROUTINES
The learner demonstrates clear instructions, an organized transition between
activities, and a respectful classroom routine. Evidence is a mentor observation
record describing the activity and feedback. No student names are needed.

C3: INCLUSIVE INSTRUCTION
The learner explains how a lesson can support different learning needs. Evidence
is an anonymized adapted lesson plan and a short reflection explaining the
changes. The mentor checks the connection between the adaptation and the need.

C4: CHECKING UNDERSTANDING
The learner uses a short assessment and explains how its results would inform
the next lesson. Evidence is an anonymized assessment example, a brief reflection,
and a mentor observation. Do not upload identifiable student work or records.

EVIDENCE WORKFLOW
The learner submits evidence against the relevant competency. The mentor reviews
it, requests changes if needed, or signs off the competency. Until sign-off, the
competency remains incomplete even when evidence has been uploaded.

FINAL CHECK
All four competencies must be signed off. The administrator also checks approved
practice hours and outstanding corrections before marking program completion.
These demo competency names are illustrative, not an official teaching standard.
